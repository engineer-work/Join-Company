# 7. KPIs & performance metrics (examples)

* **Component MTBF:** target > design life to meet 20-yr warranty (per subsystem).
* **Robot uptime:** > 99% nominal; > 95% degraded mode.
* **AI prediction accuracy:** ROC AUC > 0.9 for failure prediction; median Time-to-Failure within ±10% error for critical components.
* **Autonomous repair success rate:** > 95% for injected fault tests.
* **System survival guarantee metric:** No single event leads to mission loss in simulated scenarios up to N simultaneous failures (define N per SRS).
* **Crew health metrics:** bone density, immune markers within defined safe bands after 1, 5, 10 years.

# 8. Risks & mitigations (top 10)

1. **Insufficient funding** — mitigation: staged deliverables, partner engagements, early revenue tech spinouts.
2. **AI safety/explainability failure** — mitigation: layered safety rules, human override, formal verification of critical modules.
3. **Robotics unreliability** — mitigation: robot redundancy, modular replacement, robots repair robots.
4. **Materials failure (radiation)** — mitigation: multi-layer shielding, in-situ repairable composites.
5. **Regulatory hurdles** — mitigation: early regulator engagement, phased certification.
6. **Supplier single-point of failure** — mitigation: multiple qualified suppliers and spares.
7. **Crew health decline** — mitigation: medical automation, remote telemedicine, conservative exposure limits.
8. **Launch failures** — mitigation: multiple launch providers, abort capability, insurance.
9. **Schedule slips** — mitigation: aggressive risk management, stage gates, contingency funds.
10. **IP/Export controls** — mitigation: legal counsel, compliance program, early filings.
