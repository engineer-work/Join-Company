# 2️⃣ Humanoid Robot Repair Workflows

### Why humanoid robots?

* Human-tool compatible
* Can navigate ladders, tunnels, and tight spaces
* Flexible for unknown failures

---

## 🔧 Repair Workflow (Autonomous Loop)

### Step 1: Detection

* Sensors detect anomaly (heat, vibration, radiation, pressure)
* AI classifies severity: **Minor / Major / Critical**

---

### Step 2: Diagnosis

* Robot scans:

  * Visual (camera + lidar)
  * Structural (ultrasonic / magnetic)
  * Thermal & radiation mapping
* AI compares data with baseline models

---

### Step 3: Planning

* AI generates repair plan:

  * Tools required
  * Spare parts needed
  * Risk assessment
* Secondary AI validates the plan

---

### Step 4: Repair Execution

* Robot performs:

  * Welding / sealing
  * Component replacement
  * Cable rerouting
  * Circuit re-soldering
* Zero-gravity stabilization via magnetic boots or anchors

---

### Step 5: Verification

* System re-tested
* Stress test applied
* If failed → alternative repair path activated

---

### Step 6: Self-Repair (Robots)

* Robots can:

  * Replace their own limbs
  * Swap processors
  * Recalibrate sensors
* At least **3 robots**, so no single robot failure is fatal
