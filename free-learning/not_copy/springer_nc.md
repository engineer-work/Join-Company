### 🤖 AI and Authorship Policies
These links detail the specific rules regarding the use of Large Language Models (LLMs) like ChatGPT.

- **Nature Portfolio AI Policy (Nature Journals)**: States that LLMs do not satisfy authorship criteria and must be documented in the Methods section.
    - Source: [www.nature.com/tp/authors-and-referees/editorial-policies](https://www.nature.com/tp/authors-and-referees/editorial-policies) (See "Authorship" section)

- **Springer Nature AI Guidelines**: Summarizes the rules for AI use, including the prohibition of generative AI for image creation and disclosure requirements.
    - Source: [preview.springer.com/kr/editorial-policies/ethical-responsibilities-of-authors](https://preview.springer.com/kr/editorial-policies/ethical-responsibilities-of-authors)
